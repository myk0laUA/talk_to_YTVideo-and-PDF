# Shell visual decisions

This records the minimal bootstrap shell, not an approved final Step 05 design.

The workspace is an Operate surface with source reading to follow. Keep the source region first and conversation second; use two columns above 700 px and one below. The empty state explains unimplemented functionality. Avoid decorative imagery, animation and marketing claims during foundation work.

Tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`: paper `#f6f5ef`, surface `#ffffff`, ink `#203b35`, secondary text `#52665f`, separators `#d4ddd5`, focus accent `#245b48`. Body/control type uses locally available Segoe UI/sans-serif; the introductory heading uses Georgia/serif. No remote font fetch is required to build. Panels use 24–32 px padding, 12 px outer radius and a single border. Controls are at least 48 px high; focus uses a visible 3 px outline. No motion is currently needed.

Step 05 should shape the real source-entry/preview flow with Impeccable and the existing product plan. Bootstrap makes no claim of a complete Impeccable design audit or final visual approval.
